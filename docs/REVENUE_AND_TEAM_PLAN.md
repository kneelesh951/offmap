## Minimum Team — Hiring Roadmap

### Phase 0: Solo (Now → First €5K/month revenue)

Just you. Claude Code is your engineering co-pilot.

| Role | Who does it |
|------|------------|
| Engineering | You + Claude Code |
| Design | You (Tailwind + competitor inspiration) |
| Marketing | You (social media, Reddit, travel forums) |
| Support | You (email, <20 tickets/week at this scale) |
| Finance | You (Kleingewerbe, simple bookkeeping) |

**Key:** Don't hire until revenue justifies it. Every euro goes into product + marketing.

---

### Phase 1: First Hire (€5K–€15K/month revenue, ~1K–5K users)

**Hire #1: Community/Marketing Person (part-time, €1.5K–€2.5K/month)**

- **Why first:** You're the bottleneck on growth, not engineering. Claude Code handles dev. You need someone acquiring hosts and travelers.
- **What they do:** Social media, host recruitment in new cities, content creation, respond to user emails, manage host onboarding calls.
- **Where to find:** Working student (Werkstudent) in Germany — €12–€15/hr, 15–20hrs/week.
- This person becomes your "city launcher" — they onboard 10–20 hosts per new city.

**At this point you can consider quitting your job** if monthly revenue covers your living expenses + this hire + infra for 3 consecutive months.

---

### Phase 2: Small Team (€15K–€50K/month revenue, 5K–25K users)

**Hire #2: Full-stack Developer (part-time → full-time, €3K–€5K/month)**

- **Why:** Platform complexity grows (bookings, payouts, multi-currency). You need someone who can ship features while you focus on product direction.
- **What they do:** Feature development, bug fixes, on-call for production issues.
- **Where to find:** Remote junior-mid dev, Eastern Europe or LATAM for cost efficiency.

**Hire #3: Customer Support (part-time, €1K–€2K/month)**

- **Why:** At 5K+ users, support tickets hit 50–100/week. You and hire #1 can't handle it.
- **What they do:** Email support, dispute resolution, host quality checks.
- **Where to find:** Virtual assistant or part-time remote.

**Team budget at €50K/month revenue:**

| Role | Cost/month |
|------|-----------|
| You (founder — product, strategy, engineering) | €4K–€6K |
| Community/Marketing (full-time by now) | €3K–€4K |
| Developer | €3K–€5K |
| Support (part-time) | €1K–€2K |
| **Total payroll** | **€11K–€17K** |
| Infrastructure | €500–€2.5K |
| **Total burn** | **€12K–€20K** |
| **Profit at €50K revenue** | **€30K–€38K/month** |

## Key Hiring Principles

1. **Hire #1 is NOT an engineer** — it's a marketer/community person. Claude Code is your engineering team until €15K/month revenue.
2. **Remote-first** — access global talent at fair rates. Don't pay Munich salaries until €100K/month.
3. **Part-time → full-time** — every hire starts part-time or contract. Convert once proven.
4. **Revenue before headcount** — never hire ahead of revenue. Each hire should pay for itself within 2–3 months.
5. **You quit your job** when monthly revenue > 2× your salary for 3 consecutive months.

## Summary

- **25 distinct revenue streams** identified across 6 tiers
- **Core 3 streams** (subscriptions + bookings + host tiers) can generate €130K–€180K/month at 100K users
- **Infrastructure never exceeds 7% of revenue** at any scale
- **Team scales from 1 → 15 people** over 3 years, always trailing revenue
- **First hire is marketing, not engineering** — growth is the bottleneck, not code
- **Quit your job at 2× salary revenue** sustained for 3 months
