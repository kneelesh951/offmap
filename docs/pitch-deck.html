OUR MOAT
Network effects: every new host makes the platform more valuable to travelers, and every new traveler makes it more valuable to hosts. Switching cost grows with reviews and reputation.
WHY NOW
Post-COVID "experience economy" shift. Gen Z and Millennial travelers (65% of market) reject scripted tourism. Creator economy normalised paying for direct access to experts.
DEFENSIBILITY
Trust is hard to replicate. Our review system, verified host profiles, and cancellation policy engine create compounding data advantages that take years to build from scratch.
